Mission notes & checks

An illustrative, expendable ascent carrying a modeled 12 t Starlink stack. Target: a 200 km circular orbit, with both calculated apsides within ±25 km. Mission completion means SECO; it does not by itself mean orbital insertion. Payload deployment and booster recovery are outside this flight.

Gravity follows a spherical Earth. The atmosphere is stationary and exponential; Earth rotation, wind, lift, fuel slosh, and detailed engine control are omitted. Dry masses, fuel allocations and guidance are engineering approximations, not a reconstruction of a particular mission.

Public references: SpaceX Falcon 9 overview and Falcon User’s Guide. The modeled silhouette is 70 m tall, 3.7 m wide, with a 5.2 × 13.2 m fairing.

Proper acceleration is non-gravitational force per unit mass. It reads approximately 1 g while supported by the pad and approaches zero during a vacuum coast. Max-Q is marked when the calculated pressure peak is confirmed by a sustained decline.

Drag to orbit; scroll or +/− to zoom. Arrow keys orbit when the canvas is focused. C restores the cinematic view. Space launches or pauses; R resets; 1, 4, 0 select playback speed. Reduced motion disables shake and camera sweeps.
